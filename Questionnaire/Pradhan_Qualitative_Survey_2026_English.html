Namaste. My name is [_____] and I am here on behalf of Morsel Research and Development (Morsel India). We are helping carry out a research study on for Tanisha Mohapatra from University of Oxford, and her supervisor, Professor David Rueda at Oxford. This project is on “Inequality & Representation: Experiences and decisions of panchayat pradhans” and it is strictly for academic research purposes, and neither we, nor the researchers are part of any commercial or political group or organization.

We are speaking with many pradhans in UP, and we will ask you to describe your experience as candidates, your motivations, and how you make decisions and implement programs. There are no right or wrong answers -- we are only interested in how you see things. This study has been reviewed by Oxford’s research ethics body.

The interview will take about 30-40 minutes. We are very grateful for your time and effort. There is no payment or benefit for taking part, and there is no penalty or disadvantage if you choose not to. You can decline to answer any question, and you can stop the interview at any point, without giving any reason, without any consequence. Taking part is completely voluntary.

Your views or perspective will be kept confidential. Findings will be reported only in aggregate, anonymized form. At no point will any information that could personally identify you be released to anyone. If you take part today but change your mind later, you can contact the PI by WhatsApp or email within two months of this interview. Your responses will be deleted and left out of any further analysis. This sheet has contact information and all the details we have told you.

If you wish to give further permission, we’d like to make an audio recording of our interview. This is strictly for checking quality and ensuring that the PIs understand your views accurately. The recording is completely optional -- you can take part in the interview fully without it, you can ask us to stop at any time. Recordings can be accessed only by the PIs and supervisors and are deleted automatically two months after the interview.
